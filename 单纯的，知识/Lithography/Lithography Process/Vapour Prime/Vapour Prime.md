- The first step of photolithography
  光刻的第一步
- Usually integrated with the wafer dehydration bake on the same process
  在这一步骤中经常集成Wafer Dehydration Bake
- Primes wafer with Hexamethyldisilazane (HMDS)
  使用HMDS对晶圆进行处理
- Promotes good resist-to-wafer adhesion
  促进光刻胶和晶圆良好粘接

![[Pasted image 20250203161449.png#pic_center|由于表面污染和水分层的存在，导致光刻胶粘附不良和光刻胶脱落]]

### HMDS处理中的变化：

- HMDS turns wafer surface from hydrophilic to hydrophobic for better resist adhesion.
  HMDS将硅的表面由亲水性变为疏水性以让光刻胶良好粘接$$\text{Si-dioxide}+\mathrm{H_2O}+\text{HMDS}\rightarrow \text{Hexamethyldisiloxane}+\text{Ammonia}$$
![[Pasted image 20250203170107.png#pic_center|]]
- After dehydration baking these oxidised samples, it is important to spin coat them first with HMDS primer. The HMDS primer will bond with the oxide groups to seal out the moisture.
  脱水烘烤这些氧化样品之后，用HMDS底漆进行旋涂非常重要。HMDS底漆将与氧化物基团结合以隔绝水分
- The $\mathrm{Si(CH_3)_3}$ groups are compatible with the resist, creating adhesion between the sample and the resist.
  $\mathrm{Si(CH_3)_3}$基团与光刻胶兼容，能够在样品和光刻胶之间建立粘附力

### Typical Process Sequence

- Dehydration bake (200°C to 250°C)
- Vapour priming
  蒸汽预处理
- Priming Techniques
	- Puddle / Spray dispense and spin
	  液滴 / 喷涂分配并旋转

### Process Summary

![[Pasted image 20250203162108.png#pic_center|Enclosed Chamber with Exhaust]]

- Dehydration bake in an enclosed chamber with exhaust
  在封闭带有排气的腔室中脱水烘烤
- Hexamethyldisilazane (HMDS) prime
  使用HMDS预处理
- Exhaust
  排气
- Clean and dry wafer surface (hydrophobic)
  清洁并干燥晶圆表面（变成疏水性了）
- Temperature ~ 200°C to 250°C
- Time ~ 60 seconds
