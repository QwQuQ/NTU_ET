- Process Summary:
	- Transfers the mask image to the resist-coated wafer
	  将掩膜上的图案转移到涂有光刻胶的晶圆上
	- Activates photo-sensitive components of the resist
	  激活光刻胶的光敏成分
	- Quality measures:
		- Linewidth resolution
		  线宽分辨率
		- Overlay accuracy
		  叠层精度
		- Particles and defects
		  粒子和缺陷
