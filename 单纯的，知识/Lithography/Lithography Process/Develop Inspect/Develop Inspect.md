- Inspect to verify a quality pattern:
  检查以验证质量图案：
	- Identify quality problems (defects)
	  识别质量问题（缺陷）
	- Characterise the performance of the photolithography process by providing feedback regarding the quality of the lithography process
	  通过提供有关光刻工艺质量的反馈来表征光刻工艺的性能
	- Prevents scrap
	  防止废料产生
	- Prevents passing defects to other areas such as etching or deposition
	  防止缺陷传递到其他工艺区域，如蚀刻或沉积
- Plasma Clean: Rework on defective resist-coated wafers
  等离子清洗：对涂有光刻胶的缺陷晶圆进行返工。被Reject的Wafer会清洗后重新涂光刻胶
- Typically an automated operation
  通常为自动化操作
- Develop inspect rework flow
  研发检查与返工流程
