- In the Man-in-the-Middle Attack (MITM) (also known as an eavesdropping attack), an attacker comes in between a two-party communication, i.e., the attacker hijacks the session between a client and host.
  中间人攻击（Man-in-the-Middle Attack，MITM，也称为窃听攻击）：攻击者介入两方的通信，即劫持客户端和主机之间的会话。
- As seen below, the client-server communication has been cut off, and instead, the communication line goes through the hacker.
  在如图所示的情况下，客户端与服务器之间的通信已被切断，取而代之的是通信线路通过黑客进行。这种情况通常发生在中间人攻击（MITM）中，攻击者成功拦截了双方的通信。这是网络安全中的一种严重威胁。

- MITM attacks can be prevented by following the below-mentioned steps:
	- Be mindful of the security of the website.
	  注意网站的安全性。
	- Use encryption on your devices.
	  在您的设备上使用加密技术。
	- Refrain from using public Wi-Fi networks.
	  避免使用公共 Wi-Fi 网络。
