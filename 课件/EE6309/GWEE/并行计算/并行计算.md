- Another interconnection topology used, especially in a supercomputer interconnect, is an n-dimensional cube, also known as a hypercube. A 2-D square when logically extended to 3-D becomes a cube. As shown in Figure 3.29, a cube projected to 4-D becomes a tesseract. The nodes in a square have two edges; those in a cube have three edges and those in a tesseract have four edges. Such hypercube structures provide defined relationships between the number of nodes and the average number of hops to get to a random other node. The diameter grows with a low exponent (such as a square root) in the number of dimensions, while the number of nodes grows exponentially, such as squaring with the hypercube. A hypercube has the smallest diameter for the number of nodes.
  另一种互连拓扑，特别是在超级计算机互连中使用的是n维立方体，也称为超立方体。二维正方形在逻辑上扩展到三维时会变成一个立方体。如图所示，立方体投影到四维时会变成一个四维超立方体（tesseract）。正方形中的节点有两个边；立方体中的节点有三个边；四维超立方体中的节点有四个边。这种超立方体结构提供了节点数量与到达随机其他节点的平均跳数之间的定义关系。直径以较低的指数（例如平方根）随着维度的增加而增长，而节点数量呈指数增长，例如超立方体中的平方关系。超立方体具有节点数量最小直径。
- High-dimensionality hypercubes are not too hard to wire up at the supercomputer rack scale due to the freedom of the 3-D world. However, a silicon chip is essentially 2-D with a little bit of extension into the third dimension from multiple wiring layers. Hence, pragmatic regular on-chip topologies tend to use a torus structure with a low density of long links that span multiple mesh hops in one step. These tend to approximate a hypercube of dimensionality 2.25.
  高维超立方体在超级计算机机架规模上并不难连接，因为在三维世界中具有自由度。然而，硅芯片本质上是二维的，并且通过多层布线有少许扩展到第三维。因此，实际的片上规则拓扑通常使用环形结构，并带有低密度的长链接，这些链接在一个步骤中跨越多个网格跳跃。这些结构倾向于接近维度为2.25的超立方体结构。

# 缓存一致性

- Generally, each processor has its own cache to reduce system bus traffic.
- Cache coherence (data consistency in caches) problem cannot be solved just by using write-through procedure. One processor may update data $X$ in both its cache and main (global) memory. If $X$ is then changed by another processor, the new value of $X$ will be written into global memory but the two caches will contain different value of $X$.
  不能简单地用写穿透策略来解决，写穿透只能解决缓存与主存之间的一致性，不能解决不同处理器之间缓存的一致性。

---

解决办法：

- All writable shared data are tagged as noncacheable (i.e. can only be accessed directly from main memory). Use a write-through procedure that requires a process to mark a shared cache item as invalid, or to be de-allocated, whenever the processor writes into that item.
  所有可写的共享数据被标记为不可缓存（即只能直接从主内存访问）。采用一种Write-through程序，当处理器向共享缓存项中写入数据时，需要将该缓存项标记为无效或解除分配

- It is a hardware-based solution that requires a processor to broadcast its write operation to all caches. Each cache will check whether the broadcast item is presently in it. If it is, the cache page or line in question is updated or invalidated. (IBM S/360 – 370) (Write once).
  这是一种基于硬件的解决方案，需要处理器将其写操作广播到所有缓存。每个缓存都会检查广播的项目是否存在于其内部。如果存在，则相关的缓存页或缓存行会被更新或标记为无效。

# 性能评估

## Cycles per Instruction (CPI)

$$\text{CPI}=\frac{\sum(\text{Instruction Count}\times \text{Clock Cycle Count})}{\sum(\text{Instruction Count})}=\frac{1}{E}$$
其中$E$是Efficiency

## Instruction Bandwidth

Millions of instructions per second (MIPS): $$\text{MIPS}=\frac{f}{\text{CPI}\times10^6}$$
$f$为处理器频率

## Data Bandwidth (Throughput)

- (MFLOPS (Megaflops)): Millions of floating-point operations per second 
- (GFLOPS (Gigaflops)): Billions of floating-point operations per second 
- Execution time of a task:$$\text{Total number of clock cycles} \times \text{clock cycle time}$$

## Speedup

$$\text{speedup}=\frac{\text{Total execution time on a sequential computer}}{\text{Total execution time on the parallel computer}}$$
$$S(n)=\frac{T(1)}{T(n)}\text{, } n \text{ level (or degree) of parallelism}$$

## Efficiency

$$E(n)=\frac{\text{Effective processing time used}}{\text{Total available processing time}}= \text{The speedup per degree of parallelism}=\frac{S(n)}{n}$$

# Performance Analysis of a Parallel Computer

- For example, it is desired to add two 64-component vectors $$X=(x_0 , x_1 , \cdots, x_{63})$$ $$Y=(y_0 , y_1 , \cdots, y_{63})$$ to obtain the result $$Z = (z_0 ,z_1 , \cdots, z_{63})$$The computer first stores the $i_{th}$ components $x_i$ and $y_i$ of $X$ and $Y$ in memory locations $x_i$ and $y_i$, respectively of processor memory $M_i$ for $i=1, 2, \cdots, 63$ It then broadcast the fixed-point or floating-point add instruction $$Z\leftarrow x + y$$ to all PEs, causing 64 scalar additions to take place simultaneously. This produces the desired vector result $Z$ in just one vector addition time. Let $F$ be the fraction of all floating-point operations that are executed as scalar operations, and so $(1-F)$ be the fraction to be executed as vector operations.
  例如，假设需要将两个 64 元向量 $$X=(x_0 , x_1 , \cdots, x_{63})$$ $$Y=(y_0 , y_1 , \cdots, y_{63})$$ 相加以获得结果 $$Z = (z_0 ,z_1 , \cdots, z_{63})$$计算机首先将 $X$ 和 $Y$ 的第 $i$ 个分量 $x_i$ 和 $y_i$ 分别存储在处理器内存 $M_i$ 的内存位置 $x_i$ 和 $y_i$ 中，其中 $i=1, 2, \cdots, 63$。 然后，它向所有处理单元 (PEs) 广播固定点或浮点加法指令 $$Z\leftarrow x + y$$，从而同时进行 64 次标量加法运算。这将在一个向量加法时间内生成所需的向量结果 $Z$。设 $F$ 为以标量操作执行的浮点运算的比例，则 $(1-F)$ 为以向量操作执行的比例。

---

- 令$b_V$为向量操作的吞吐量（一秒内产生标量操作的次数）
- 那么$$\frac{1}{b_V}$$就是在向量环境中进行一次标量操作的平均时间
- 所以一次向量操作的时间为：$$T_V=\frac{N}{b_V}$$

- 令$b_S$是标量操作的吞吐量，那么系统总的吞吐量为：$$\frac{1}{b}=\frac{F}{b_S}+\frac{1-F}{b_V}$$
- 对于一个$N$元素向量操作而言，执行时间是：$$T_V=\frac{N}{b_V}$$
- 一个标量操作的时间是：$$T_S=\frac{1}{b_S}$$
- 假设向量操作中的标量和普通的标量是相同的，加上分发数据什么的时间和并行度后，等式会变为：$$T_V=T_o+\frac{NT_S}{n}$$
  其中：
	- $n$为处理器并行度
	- $T_o$为一个固定的启动时间
- 当$N$足够大，可以把$T_o$忽略，从而得到$$T_V\approx\frac{NT_S}{n}$$
- 带入上述式子后得到：$$\frac{N}{b_V}=\frac{N}{n\times b_S}\implies b_V=n\times b_S$$
- 所以系统的吞吐量为：$$\frac{1}{b}=\frac{F}{b_S}+\frac{1-F}{n\times b_S}\implies b=\frac{nb_S}{1+\left(n-1\right)F}$$
  其中：
	- $b_S$和$n$可以被视为常数
- 能够发现标量操作越多，系统吞吐量越低

---

- Speedup推导：$$S(n)=\frac{1/b_S}{1/b}=\frac{b}{b_S}$$
- 代入$b=\frac{nb_S}{1+\left(n-1\right)F}$，得到：$$S(n)=\frac{n}{1+(n-1)F}$$
- 上面这个式子是Amdahl’s Law

# Performance Analysis of a Pipeline Supercomputer

- Pipelining depends on the ability to break an operation into sub-operations that are processed sequentially by stages. The activity of a simple pipeline is illustrated as below:
![[Pasted image 20250415011935.png#pic_50center|]]

- The operands for the operation are introduced at stage 1 and, when it completes its suboperation, it passes its result on to stage 2, which then begins its suboperation. At this time, stage 1 may accept operands for a second operation, even though the first operation is still in process. After both stages 1 and 2 have completed their suboperations, stage 3 can begin operating on the output of stage 2, stage 2 can begin operating on the output of stage 1, and stage 1 can accept a third set of operands. This process can continue indefinitely.
  操作数首先在第1级被引入，当第1级完成其子操作后，它会将结果传递给第2级，第2级随之开始其子操作。与此同时，第1级可以接受第2次操作的操作数，即使第1次操作仍在进行中。当第1级和第2级都完成了各自的子操作后，第3级可以开始处理第2级的输出，第2级可以开始处理第1级的输出，而第1级可以接受第3组操作数。这一过程可以无限地继续。

---

- Assume that an operation by a non-pipeline sequential machine can be sub-divided into (or consists of) 3 sub-operations.
![[Pasted image 20250415010131.png#pic_50center|]]
- 如果$T_ S$是执行一段操作的时间，那么$$T_S=T_{p1}+T_{p2}+T_{p3}$$如果每个sub-operation的操作时间相同，为$T_p$，可以得到$$T_S=3T_p$$（这不废话吗）

---

- 考虑一个$M$级流水线，每一级的时间都是$T_p$
- 那么做完$N$个完整操作的时间是$$T=\left(M+N-1\right)T_p$$
- 吞吐量提升是：$$S(n)=\frac{M\times N}{M+N-1}$$
- 流水线中所有可用的时间为（包括IDLE的模块）：$$M(N+M-1)T_p$$
- 流水线中有效的处理时间为（不包括IDLE的模块）：$$(N\times M)T_p$$
## Pipeline Efficiency

![[Pasted image 20250415012332.png#pic_75center|流水线的效率]]
- 流水线的效率为：$$E=\frac{N}{N+M-1}$$

- 讨论$M\uparrow,S(n)\uparrow$，$M$提高也会提高Speedup，因为能够在时间上叠加更多
- 讨论$M\uparrow,E\downarrow$，提高$M$会让效率下降，因为需要更长的时间启动流水线。

- It has been tacitly assume that all stages require the same processing time. To keep the pipeline fully utilized, it must work in a lock-step fashion with all stages using the same amount of time, and this time must be the maximum time taken over the set of all stages. If one stage requires less time to complete its work then the remaining is wasted. It is, therefore important to design the stages so that they require approximately the same processing time.
  已默认为所有阶段需要相同的处理时间。为了使流水线充分利用，它必须以lock-step方式工作，所有阶段使用相同的时间，而该时间必须是所有阶段中所需时间的最大值。如果一个阶段需要更少的时间完成其工作，那么剩余的时间就会被浪费。因此，设计阶段时必须确保它们大致需要相同的处理时间。
## Pipeline Throughput

- For M-stage and N operations, we have ($T_p$ is pipeline clock): $$b_p=\frac{N}{(M+N-1)T_p}$$
- 讨论$T_p \downarrow, b\uparrow$：When $T_p$ is decreased, the throughput will be increased. One way to decrease $T_p$ is to use **duplicate hardware**.
![[Pasted image 20250415013305.png#pic_50center|Duplicate Hardware]]

---

- An alternative method is by dividing those stages requiring the most time into more than one stage.
  另一种方法是将耗时最多的阶段分为多个阶段。
![[Pasted image 20250415013551.png#pic_50center|]]
- If by adding $p$ new stages, $T_p$ is decreased by the factor $1/c,\ c>1$, then, the throughput becomes:$$b=\frac{N}{(N+M+P-1)T_p}c\implies \lim_{N\rightarrow \infty}b=\frac{c}{T_p}$$

## Typical Pipeline Multiprocessing Configuration

- In this configuration, several pipelined processing elements have been placed in parallel. Theoretically, if there are $N$ processors, each with $M$ stages, and each processor requires $T$ seconds to process the $M$ stages, then the throughput could approach $\frac{NM}{T}$ operations per second as the number of operations in a task goes to infinity.
  在这种配置中，多个流水线处理单元被并行放置。理论上，如果有 $N$ 个处理器，每个处理器具有 $M$ 级流水线，并且每个处理器需要 $T$ 秒来完成这 $M$ 级的处理，那么随着任务中操作数量趋于无穷大，其吞吐量可能接近 $\frac{NM}{T}$ 次操作每秒。

# 超标量和超流水线

![[Pasted image 20250415015533.png#pic_75center|]]

- The primary goal of a RISC processor is to achieve single-cycle operation. All initial RISC processors achieve execution speeds that approach one instruction per system clock cycle. To further on this performance, two classes of processors have evolved to offer execution of multiple instructions per clock cycle:
  RISC处理器的主要目标是实现单周期操作。所有早期的RISC处理器都能达到接近每个系统时钟周期执行一条指令的速度。为了进一步提升性能，已经发展出两类处理器，可以在每个时钟周期内执行多条指令：
	- Superscalar processors are a subclass of RISC processors which allow multiple instructions to be issued concurrently within each cycle. For a superscalar pipeline, essentially it replicates each of the pipeline stages so that two or more instructions at the same stage of the pipeline can be processed simultaneously, usually with low CPI, i.e. In high-performance computing (HPC) and supercomputing, the goal is to have a low CPI, meaning more instructions are executed per clock cycle, leading to higher efficiency.
	  超标量处理器是RISC处理器的一个子类，允许在每个周期内同时发出多条指令。对于超标量流水线来说，它本质上是对每个流水线阶段进行复制，使得两条或更多指令可以在流水线的同一阶段同时处理。通常情况下，超标量处理器具有较低的CPI（每指令周期）。在高性能计算（HPC）和超级计算领域，目标是实现低CPI，这意味着每个时钟周期内执行更多指令，从而提高效率。
	- Superpipelined processors are those use multiphase clocks with an increased clock rate. Essentially, they make use of more, and more finegrained stages.
	  超流水线处理器是指使用多相时钟并提高时钟频率的处理器。本质上，它们通过增加更多、更精细的阶段来工作。

- Superscalar processors are better suited for workloads with high instruction-level parallelism (ILP), as they can issue multiple instructions per cycle. However, they require complex scheduling and dependency management.
  超标量处理器更适合具有高指令级并行性（ILP）的工作负载，因为它们每个周期可以发出多条指令。然而，它们需要复杂的调度和依赖性管理。
- Super-pipelined processors rely on higher clock speeds and deeper pipelines but may face increased stalls and branch penalties.
  超流水线处理器依赖于更高的时钟速度和更深的流水线，但可能会遇到更多的停顿和分支惩罚。
